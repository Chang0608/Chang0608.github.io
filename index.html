Most bugs I have chased did not live inside a component. They lived between components, where one part's ideal assumptions meet another part's real behavior.

So the question behind all of my work is the same: does what holds under ideal conditions still hold under real ones? Between a simulator and a robot, between an offline benchmark and a live system, between a desktop GPU and a phone.

Autonomous mower · 2025–2026

A remote-controlled mower, a production ROS stack, and a team new to both. The robot drove diagonally while every sensor looked fine; the fault was between them. The next year I turned that lesson into calibration, safety, and planning tools, still on real hardware only. That is why I build digital twins.
