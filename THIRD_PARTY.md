# Third-party assets

The background on the home page draws two robots from simplified versions of their published CAD meshes
(`robots_mesh.bin`, `robots_mesh.json`). The meshes were decimated with meshoptimizer; nothing else was changed.

| Model | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| SO-101 robot arm | [TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100) (`Simulation/SO101`) | Apache License 2.0 |
| TurtleBot3 Waffle Pi | [ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3](https://github.com/ROBOTIS-GIT/turtlebot3) (`turtlebot3_description`) | Apache License 2.0 |
